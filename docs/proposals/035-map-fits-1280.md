## Opportunity
At 1280x800, with four lead trees or blocks plus the routines column, the map scrolls sideways by 8px: `scrollWidth=1288`, `svgWidth=1276` against a `mapWidth` of 1256, and `?check=1`'s title reads `SCROLLS`. 028's lead measured it with 028's seed and with the seed before it, so it predates 028; 028 was tooling only and left it. The layout in `internal/web/map.js` (the block placement and the routines column, `colW`, around line 245) adds its columns without checking them against the page width.

## Proposal
In: the horizontal layout never makes the SVG wider than the map's width. When the blocks plus the routines column don't fit, the blocks wrap to a new row (the same wrap a narrower page already gets) rather than pushing the routines column past the edge; the lead picks between that and narrowing the gaps after measuring, and says which in the landing commit. The 390x844 layout is unchanged.

Out: new layouts, a zoom control, changes to box contents.

Judge: opus, because the wrap has to hold for any count of trees, not just this seed's.

## What you'll see
`?check=1` at 1280x800 reads `ok` with this seed and with one more lead tree than it holds.

## Verification
The repo's verify, plus the browser check from 028 (`scripts/check.mjs` and `seed.sh`, see README's testing section) at 1280x800 and 390x844: no `SCROLLS`, with the seed as it is and with two extra lead trees.

## Risks and the alternative considered
Wrapping moves boxes an operator is used to finding in one row; narrowing the gaps alone buys a few pixels and fails again at the next tree.
