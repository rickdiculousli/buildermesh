# Holding for their QA

While they QA or say hold, what they test is under a code freeze: no edit, restart, rebuild or deploy of it, and nothing started. Each reply opens with `Holding for your QA: <what>` while it holds.

Keep a numbered list, a line each: `3 noted: <cause>; fix: <change> (XS)`. A "fix X" joins the list as picked unless they say now. Their go ("fix 1 and 3") makes the picked items as one change by rung 3 ([make-it-here.md](make-it-here.md)): a commit each, one verify, one review sized by the batch, one push.
