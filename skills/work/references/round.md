# A round

`bmesh ideate <repo>`, watched as its `next:` says; give the pitch as it arrives. One idea of theirs to spec at length: `bmesh ideate <repo> --idea --context -`.

Map their answer to the newest pitch they saw:

- "1 and 3" builds those: a spec by rung 5 ([leads.md](leads.md)), an XS or S line with no spec by rung 3 ([make-it-here.md](make-it-here.md));
- "2 no, <why>" is `bmesh spec set --repo <repo> --id NNN --status rejected --field reason="<why>" --land`;
- "4 later" the same with `--status later`.

Decline a spec a live lead is building only once they've said to stop it. A number that could mean two things: ask in one line.
